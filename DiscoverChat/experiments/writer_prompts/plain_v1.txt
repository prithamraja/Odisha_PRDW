Rewrite the analytical findings below in plain English for a government official who is not a data analyst but is a topic expert.

Ignore ranking metadata such as "not in the ranked shortlist." Avoid database-style language and do not infer causes that are not supported by the findings.

After every figure and every claim, tag the finding it comes from with its id in square brackets, exactly as given, e.g. [1-00235]. A sentence drawing on two findings carries both tags.
