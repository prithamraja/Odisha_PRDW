The short answer

Cuttack's two sampled panchayats planned 1,104 activities worth Rs 5.35 crore. Recorded spending is Rs 3.18 crore below that plan, and almost all of the gap sits in 923 rows with no sanctioning authority on file. Fix the records first, then read the spending.

3 · Spending against the plan

Recorded spending is Rs 3.18 crore below plan

Roads accounts for 31.0% of the shortfall (Rs 98.42 lakh) and Sanitation for 21.4% (Rs 68.08 lakh). Over six years, far more of the spending has been linked to a planned activity.

Needs checking in the next review

Missing records explain most of the shortfall

1.
923 rows have no sanctioning authority. They make up 94.1% of the shortfall against plan (Rs 2.99 crore).
2.
923 rows have no scheme details. As a result, all 9,165 expected beneficiaries sit under "not recorded".
3.
Spending against sanction is Rs 18.73 lakh below. Treat this with care: only about one activity in six has a sanction record on file.
