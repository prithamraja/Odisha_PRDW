Self-sufficient Infrastructure in Village was Rs 20.13 crore below plan across 16 blocks, with the largest gaps in Sheragada (Rs 3.36 crore) and Rangeilunda (Rs 3.11 crore). Clean and Green Village was Rs 8.62 crore below plan across 20 Gram Panchayats, with the largest gap in Biswamathpur (Rs 2.15 crore). Rural housing is a more focused check: Boipariguda accounted for 95.7% of the sample’s rural housing shortfall (Rs 2.13 crore). In Boipariguda’s own records, rural housing and drinking water were the two largest gaps, with drinking water Rs 1.26 crore below plan.

For Village with Good Governance, check the Buildings entries and reconcile records with no sanctioning authority on file. Buildings accounted for Rs 1.66 crore of the theme’s Rs 2.41 crore below plan. In a separate breakdown of the same shortfall, 1,681 records without a sanctioning authority on file accounted for Rs 2.20 crore below plan.

Check the cashbook before treating more spending linked to plans as more spending overall. The linked share rose from 2.7% to 53.2% over six years, and linked spending rose over successive quarters in 6/9 districts, with different patterns in Bargarh, Koraput and Cuttack. Although 8/9 measures were unusually high in 2026-Q1, spending linked to planned activities was the exception.
